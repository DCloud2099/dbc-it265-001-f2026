# IT265 Module 2: Peer Feedback

Use one copy for each concept you review. Keep actual workshop notes together in this document, including feedback you received when available. Initials are enough; do not record classmates' contact details. If feedback was unavailable, briefly say so instead of inventing it. Give feedback on the idea and name a decision the designer can make next.

**Concept:** Tower Tumbler

**Reviewer initials:** GC

**In my words, the player repeatedly:** builds their tower while the others build theirs. In addition to defending their tower from attacks from the other players. 

**One clarifying question:** How are the players going to build up their towers? Is it through specific spaces, casually, or how else?

**Strongest hook or source of appeal:** The "card game" aspect of the game is a good hook, since it feels like card games are a big thing nowadays in the sphere of games. 

**What one student could prototype first:** N/A

**Biggest uncertainty or risk to test:** Mainly the assets for the game, as to how one would convey the models of the game in a physical sense. That is, how will the towers look? How will they be constructed? Would a tower defense inspired game function well when there's more than 2 players?

**One actionable suggestion:** Try and figure out what kind of things would be used to build the towers. Would they all progress after every turn is complete? Is it only by cards or spaces?

Give your notes to the designer. Designers can use the feedback in the [selection sheet](./04-select-and-scope.html) and journal entry. Link this document's rendered page from your workshop index.
