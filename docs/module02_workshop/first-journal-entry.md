## Concept and Decision

**Current concept and intended experience:** Boardgame concept, in the vein of Monopoly where certain spaces cause events or give players cards that in turn cause more events

**Design question or decision:** The board then would be a closed loop, such as a square, circle, diamond, etc., and every player would begin at the same spot for fairness

**Selected concept and alternatives kept:** A keep-away inspired board game with Monopoly-like concepts (i.e. cards causing events, etc.); alternative, tower-defense inspired concept, where players will rush to complete their tower first and sabotage other players towers in the process (also with Monopoly aspects/concepts)

**Peer feedback that affected the decision, if received (otherwise say unavailable):** Chosen concept was simpler, more concise/clear on the goal for the players than the secondary option was; easy to understand, and fun to play while prolonging the game
