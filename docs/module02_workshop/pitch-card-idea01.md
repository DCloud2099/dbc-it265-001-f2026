# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.html) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:** Finders Keepers

**Player role and situation:** Players are tasked with moving towards and acquiring a particular special item before the other players acquire it first.

**Repeated decision or action:** Upon collecting the item, they must hold onto it for as long as they can until the turn ends, and repeatedly do so with each following turn. Holding onto the piece after a turn ends grants the player 1 point

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** The goal is to reach 21 points before any other player is able to. Other players in turn will attempt to steal the piece from you, and in turn you must steal it back from whoever holds it. There will be spaces and/or cards that will either benefit or hinder you, or do the same for other players

**Hook:** You can either avoid and evade, keep it to yourself for as long as possible. Or, sabotage other players! End the game quickly, or prolong it for as long as possible. The choice is yours!

**Smallest useful physical prototype:** Cardboard, small items for game pieces, markers/pencils (as well as other craft items), posterboard, dice/die

**Question I want listeners to answer:** Should there be a set amount of points to reach? Should that decision be made up to the players at the start of the game? What kind of disadvantages and advantages would be fair to have in the game itself?

Keep the cards together in this document and link its rendered page from your workshop index. They support the journal and treatment rather than adding another graded submission.
