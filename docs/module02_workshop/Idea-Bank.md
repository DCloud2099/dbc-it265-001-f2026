# IT265 Module 2: Idea Bank

Use this sheet during the concept workshop. Link the completed document from your workshop index; it supports the journal and treatment rather than adding another graded submission.

List possible concepts before choosing one. Mark each as **ready to pitch**, **unclear loop**, **scope risk**, or **save for later**. Shortlist two or three ideas for the pitch round.

| Working title | Repeated player decision or action | Category | Reason or open question |

| Finders Keepers | Players will have to work to acquire a special item that grants points to them at the end of every turn, and to avoid losing it to other players | Board Game | The gameplay loop is both frustrating and enjoyable, as is most games that one would typically play with friends or family; i.e., it invites competition | **ready to pitch**  
| Guess The Deck | Players will do their best to acquire the same set of cards they started with, while passing cards left or right, or trading/losing cards | Card Game | How will players know which direction to swap (left or right?) and how will trading/losing cards be determined? | **unclear loop**  
| Trasher Tower | Players must construct their castle first before other players while you work to sabotage their work, and they yours | board game | How will the game be situated, and how will players build their towers? | **ready to pitch**
